Quantum entanglement of two wavepackets

Six linked visualizations of the spin singlet |Ψ⁻⟩ = (|↑↓⟩ − |↓↑⟩)/√2 and its spatial cousin — running from an artistic composite, through the rigorous two-particle density and Bell statistics, to an exact free-particle Schrödinger evolution. The clouds and their anti-correlated spins are physically exact; the connecting "thread" is the metaphor, and each section makes clear which is which.

2 · Exact

The two-particle joint density

This is the rigorous version of the thread. For the correlated Gaussian (EPR-type) state, the joint density is ρ(x₁,x₂) ∝ exp[ −(x₁² − 2r·x₁x₂ + x₂²) / (2(1−r²)) ]. Slide the spatial correlation r toward −1 and the probability mass collapses onto the dashed anti-diagonal x₁ = −x₂; at r = 0 it is a round, factorizable product state. The teal/pink side curves are the single-particle marginals — they never move as you change r, which is why entanglement is invisible locally. The toggle compares the true joint density with the product of its marginals; that they differ is non-separability.

3 · Exact (Monte Carlo)

Bell correlation on the singlet

Set the two detector angles θ_A and θ_B and fire trials. Each trial draws A = ±1 fairly, then sets B opposite with probability cos²(Δθ/2) — the exact singlet prediction. The empirical anti-correlation probability (teal dots) converges onto the blue curve; sweep the dials to trace it out. The dashed line is the best linear local-realist model pinned to the same endpoints, so the gap between curve and line is the region no local hidden-variable theory can reach.

4 · Exact (Monte Carlo)

CHSH inequality and Tsirelson's bound

The full Bell protocol: each trial randomly selects one of Alice's two settings (a, a′) and one of Bob's two (b, b′), and the four correlation terms combine into S = E(a,b) − E(a,b′) + E(a′,b) + E(a′,b′). With the default optimal angles (0°, 90° for Alice; 45°, 135° for Bob), |S| climbs straight through the classical limit of 2 and settles at Tsirelson's bound 2√2 ≈ 2.83. Drag the angles together and it drops back below 2.

6 · Exact (Schrödinger)

Exact free-particle evolution

A genuine solution of the time-dependent Schrödinger equation (units ℏ = m = 1). Each wavepacket's center moves ballistically while its width spreads as σ(t) = σ₀√(1 + (ℏt/2mσ₀²)²), and the configuration-space density is the exact |Ψ(x₁,x₂,t)|² for Ψ ∝ φ_L(x₁)φ_R(x₂) + φ_R(x₁)φ_L(x₂) — computed from the full complex wavefunction. Note the consequence: showing real entanglement forces each particle to be in a superposition of going left and right (left panel), so its own density splits both ways. At early times the anti-correlated momenta show up as genuine interference fringes; they then resolve into two lobes on the anti-diagonal. The spin correlation stays fixed at E = −1.00 throughout.
