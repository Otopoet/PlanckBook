Measurement Outcome Probabilities

After the VQE optimiser converges, the circuit is measured. The true ground state is |00⟩ (probability 1), but every shot passes through an imperfect detector — each qubit is misread ~3% of the time — so a handful of shots scatter into the spin-mismatch bins. Re-sample to watch the statistical jitter; the dominant |00⟩ bar stays put.
