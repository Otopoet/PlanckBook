Hardware-Efficient Ansatz Circuit

The parameterised quantum circuit that prepares trial states for H₂. Two qubits, two rotation gates, one entangling gate. The classical optimiser tunes θ₁ and θ₂; the quantum processor evaluates the energy.

|ψ(θ)⟩ = CNOT · [Ry(θ₁) ⊗ Ry(θ₂)] · |00⟩  ·  Hardware-efficient ansatz for 2-qubit H₂
Ry(θ) ≡ exp(−iθY/2)
Single-qubit rotation about the Y-axis by angle θ. Creates superposition states from computational basis inputs.
CNOT — controlled-NOT
Flips the target qubit (q₁) if and only if the control qubit (q₀) is |1⟩. Generates entanglement between qubits.
θ₁, θ₂ — variational params
The classical optimiser adjusts these two angles each iteration to minimise the measured expectation value ⟨H⟩.
Measurement basis
Pauli terms of H are measured separately (Z, ZZ, XX, YY) and combined classically to estimate E(θ).
