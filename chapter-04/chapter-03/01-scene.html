The artistic scene

Two Gaussian wavepackets touch, interact, and drift apart while a faint thread marks their non-separability. The arrows are always anti-correlated; before measurement they shimmer between ↑ and ↓. Press Measure left to collapse the joint state into |↑↓⟩ or |↓↑⟩ — both clouds resolve at once, however far apart. The thread is the one metaphorical element: a correlation has no location in space.
