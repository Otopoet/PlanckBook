Bell correlation on the singlet

Set the two detector angles and fire trials. Each trial draws A = ±1 fairly, then sets B opposite with probability cos²(Δθ/2) — the exact singlet prediction. The empirical anti-correlation probability (teal dots) converges onto the blue curve; sweep the dials to trace it out. The dashed line is the best linear local-realist model pinned to the same endpoints, so the gap between curve and line is the region no local hidden-variable theory can reach.
