Exact free-particle evolution

A genuine solution of the time-dependent Schrödinger equation (units ℏ = m = 1). Each wavepacket's center moves ballistically while its width spreads as σ(t) = σ₀√(1 + (ℏt/2mσ₀²)²), and the configuration-space density is the exact |Ψ(x₁,x₂,t)|² for Ψ ∝ φ_L(x₁)φ_R(x₂) + φ_R(x₁)φ_L(x₂). The consequence: showing real entanglement forces each particle into a superposition of going left and right (left panel), so its own density splits both ways. Early on the anti-correlated momenta appear as genuine interference fringes; they then resolve into two anti-diagonal lobes. The spin correlation stays fixed at E = −1.00 throughout.
