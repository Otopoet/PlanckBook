Voronoi Tessellation

What is it? A space-filling foam where each polyhedral cell is the region of all points closer to one seed point than any other.

Key Property: By mathematical construction, there are no gaps. The cells fill 3D space completely.

Vi = {x ∈ ℝ³ : ||x - ri|| ≤ ||x - rj|| for all j ≠ i}

Red dots are seed points. A simplified visualisation of spatial partitioning — convex cells are scattered around each seed to convey the idea, not an exact Voronoi computation.
