CHSH inequality and Tsirelson's bound

The full Bell protocol: each trial randomly selects one of Alice's two settings (a, a′) and one of Bob's two (b, b′), and the four correlation terms combine into S = E(a,b) − E(a,b′) + E(a′,b) + E(a′,b′). With the default optimal angles (0°, 90° for Alice; 45°, 135° for Bob), |S| climbs through the classical limit of 2 and settles at Tsirelson's bound 2√2 ≈ 2.83. Drag the angles together and it drops back below 2.
