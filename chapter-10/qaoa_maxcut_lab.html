Under the hood. Cost H_C = Σ ½(1 − Z_i Z_j) over edges (it counts cut edges) · Mixer H_M = Σ X_i · State |γ,β⟩ = Π_k e^{−iβ_k H_M} e^{−iγ_k H_C} |+⟩^⊗n. The browser holds the full 2ⁿ-amplitude state vector and squares it for these bars.
Try this: start at p = 1 and sweep γ alone — watch whole families of bars rise and fall as phases interfere. Then nudge β. Hit Auto-optimise to let a gradient-free classical optimiser (coordinate descent) find good angles — that back-and-forth between your browser’s “quantum cloud” and the classical optimiser is the hybrid QAOA loop. Add a second layer and optimise again to sharpen the peak further.
