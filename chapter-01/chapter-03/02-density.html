The two-particle joint density

The rigorous version of the thread. For the correlated Gaussian (EPR-type) state, ρ(x₁,x₂) ∝ exp[ −(x₁² − 2r·x₁x₂ + x₂²) / (2(1−r²)) ]. Slide the spatial correlation r toward −1 and the mass collapses onto the dashed anti-diagonal x₁ = −x₂; at r = 0 it is a round, factorizable product state. The side curves are the marginals — they never move as r changes, so entanglement is invisible locally. The toggle compares the joint density with the product of marginals; that they differ is non-separability.
