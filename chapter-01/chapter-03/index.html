◂ PLANCK

Quantum entanglement of two wavepackets

Six standalone visualizations of the spin singlet |Ψ⁻⟩ = (|↑↓⟩ − |↓↑⟩)/√2 and its spatial cousin — from an artistic composite, through the rigorous two-particle density and Bell statistics, to an exact free-particle Schrödinger evolution. Each opens on its own.

1 · Illustrative
The artistic scene

Two clouds touch, interact, and drift apart, connected by a metaphorical thread; measure a spin to collapse the pair into anti-correlation.

 2 · Exact
The two-particle joint density |Ψ(x₁,x₂)|²

The rigorous thread: probability mass collapsing onto the anti-diagonal as the spatial correlation r → −1, with fixed marginals.

 3 · Exact (Monte Carlo)
Bell correlation on the singlet

Empirical P(anti) converging onto cos²(Δθ/2), bowing above the best local-realist line.

 4 · Exact (Monte Carlo)
CHSH inequality & Tsirelson's bound

|S| climbing past the classical limit of 2 toward 2√2 ≈ 2.83 with optimal detector angles.

 5 · Schematic clock
Coupled real & configuration space

One clock drives both pictures: clouds separate while the joint density splits into two anti-diagonal lobes.

 6 · Exact (Schrödinger)
Exact free-particle evolution

A genuine Schrödinger trajectory: ballistic separation plus σ(t) spreading, with real momentum interference fringes.
