The mixed state

A perfectly known qubit is a pure state living on the surface of the Bloch sphere. Add noise, ignorance, or entanglement with the environment and the state slips inside the ball — it becomes mixed. The density matrix ρ captures both regimes at once: its diagonal holds the populations (probabilities), its off-diagonal the coherences (the fragile "quantumness"). Decoherence is simply those off-diagonals fading to zero. Drag the sliders and watch the tip leave the surface.

Diagonal = populations — how likely you are to measure |0⟩ or |1⟩.   Off-diagonal = coherences — the quantumness. Decoherence shrinks these to zero.

Inside the ball = mixed. A pure state sits exactly on the surface (|r|=1). As coherences fade, the tip retreats toward the centre, where the state is maximally mixed — a classical coin flip with no quantum information left.
