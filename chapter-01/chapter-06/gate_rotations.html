Gates as Bloch-sphere rotations

Every single-qubit gate is just a rigid rotation of the Bloch vector about a fixed axis — nothing more. Click a gate to watch the state swing into place, or build a sequence and run it gate by gate. Try the preset H → Z: it carries |0⟩ to |+⟩ and then to |−⟩.
