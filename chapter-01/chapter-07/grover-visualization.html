All four states begin at the same positive height 1/√N. The oracle Uω recognises the target and flips its phase — the marked bar is driven negative while every other bar is left untouched. Nothing else changes; only a sign is planted.

The Diffuser Formation Inversion

The diffuser D reflects every amplitude about the mean ⟨α⟩. Because the marked bar is the lone negative value, it sits far below the average — so its reflection vaults it high above the others, while the unmarked bars shrink toward the line.

D = 2|ψ⟩⟨ψ| − I   ⟹   αx → 2⟨α⟩ − αx

The Grover Amplification Sequence

Each Grover iteration G = D·Uω is one "mark‑then‑reflect" cycle. Repeat it and the target's probability climbs toward 1, peaking near the optimal count ⌊π√N⁄4⌋ — push past that and it over‑rotates back down.

G = D · Uω   ·   |ψk⟩ = Gk|ψ⟩   ·   P(ω) = sin²((2k+1)θ),  sinθ = 1/√N
