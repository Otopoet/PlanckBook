Bloch Sphere — Spin-½ Superposition

A "smeared arrow" that points up and down at once, until you measure it.
|ψ⟩ = 0.92|↑⟩ + ei·0.00 0.38|↓⟩
amplitudes = cos(θ/2) and eiφ·sin(θ/2)
Born rule — probability of each outcome
↑ up
85%
↓ down
15%
P(↑) = cos²(θ/2)  ·  P(↓) = sin²(θ/2)
Measured: ↑ 0   ↓ 0
The purple cloud is the superposition: the spin has no definite direction yet — only a distribution of possibilities. Drag θ to change the odds (the bars), and φ to change the quantum phase (watch the arrow sweep while the bars stay fixed). Hit Measure and the cloud collapses: the arrow snaps to ↑ or ↓ at random, weighted by the Born rule. Measure repeatedly and the tally converges to the percentages.
